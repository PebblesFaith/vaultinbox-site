National Mail Delivery Reliability Study

VaultInbox.cloud is conducting an independent research study on mail delivery reliability and dispute resolution challenges. This survey is not affiliated with the United States Postal Service (USPS).

Privacy + scope

Please do not submit addresses, tracking numbers, names of individuals, or other sensitive personal information. Responses are analyzed in aggregate to understand failure modes and willingness-to-pay for tamper-evident digital proof.

Each response is tagged with a unique Submission ID (UUID) for research integrity.
